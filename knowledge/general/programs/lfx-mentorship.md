# Understand the program

LFX Mentorship connects eligible mentees with Linux Foundation project mentors for defined projects. Terms, projects, stipends, eligibility, hours and timelines can change. Use the current LFX Mentorship portal and official documentation rather than old lists or community predictions.

# Build project fit

Choose a project because its purpose and technical area fit you, not only because it appeared in a previous term. Read the repository, governance, contributor guide, issue history and meeting notes. Join the official channel and observe how decisions are made.

Make ordinary contributions when useful. Contributions, review responsiveness, research and community participation can demonstrate readiness, but selection criteria differ by project. Never assume a fixed pull request count.

# Apply with evidence

Follow the exact project application instructions. Explain your motivation, relevant experience, understanding of the proposed work, implementation approach, milestones, testing plan, availability and learning goals. Be honest about gaps and show how you will close them.

Research before asking, then ask peers or mentors focused questions. Strong mentees communicate early, take responsibility, accept feedback and report blockers before deadlines.

# During mentorship

Agree on communication rhythm, deliverables and definitions of done. Share concise progress, decisions, next steps and blockers. Keep work visible through issues and pull requests. Test thoroughly and document the result so the project can maintain it after the term.

# After rejection or completion

Selection depends on project priorities, mentor availability and applicant fit. Continue participating if the community is healthy and the work remains valuable. After completion, help future contributors and remain involved when possible.
