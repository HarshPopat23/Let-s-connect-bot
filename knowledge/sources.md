# WhatsApp synthesis policy

The repository was informed by recurring questions and reusable principles in an organizer-provided community export. The raw export, phone numbers, contact file, participant names, private conversations, jokes, disputes and unverified claims are not included.

Only broad guidance was retained, including collaboration over competition, beginner questions being welcome, consistency over appearances, understanding why an issue matters, using AI as a learning aid, preferring quality over contribution count, respecting maintainer time and avoiding unsolicited private messages.

Community-derived guidance was checked against primary public sources wherever possible. It is not presented as a direct quotation or endorsement by an individual participant.
