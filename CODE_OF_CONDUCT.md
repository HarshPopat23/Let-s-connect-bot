# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We commit to a welcoming, safe and professional environment for people of all backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expected behavior

- Demonstrate empathy and kindness.
- Respect different opinions, experiences and identities.
- Give and accept constructive technical feedback.
- Acknowledge mistakes and repair harm.
- Focus on the health of the project and community.
- Make space for beginner questions without humiliation.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, sexualized attention, threats, doxxing, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, repeated unwanted contact and publishing private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions and communication that violate this code. Serious or repeated violations may result in temporary or permanent exclusion.

For a private report, contact an OSS Let's Connect organizer through the existing private community admin channel. Do not report sensitive conduct incidents in a public GitHub issue. Maintainers must publish a dedicated private reporting address before inviting participation outside the existing community.

This policy is informed by Contributor Covenant version 2.1:
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
